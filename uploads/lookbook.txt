About MBRIF
The Mohammed Bin Rashid Innovation Fund (MBRIF) is a
federal initiative launched by the UAE Ministry of Finance
(MoF). The initiative seeks to identify and nurture high-
potential innovations both within the UAE and abroad. It aims
to enhance the growth potential of businesses and shape the
future of the UAE’s economy.
MBRIF focuses on the seven sectors outlined in the UAE
National Innovation Strategy: Space, Transport, Education,
Technology, Water, Clean Energy, and Health.
Please see below for more information on the members
presenting.
